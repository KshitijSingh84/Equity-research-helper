## Key Risks
The main risk is demand and order timing: the government has not floated anti-drone or simulator tenders, so order inflow is soft. Other concerns are margin pressure from fixed-cost absorption on low revenue, a working capital cycle of 257 days, expected competition, and long gestation (2-3 years) for robotics and autonomous products.

## Key Analyst Question
Gautam Rathi (CWC) asked why order flow is soft despite Zen's expansion into five capabilities, and whether the cause is internal or external. Management answered that it is a demand issue, not a supply or technology issue. No anti-drone or simulator tenders have been floated, but fast-track tenders are expected to come and be finalised within months. Simulators and anti-drone systems will drive the next 1-2 years, and robotics and autonomous vehicles will take 2-3 years to mature.
