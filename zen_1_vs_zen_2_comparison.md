# Earnings call comparison: zen_1 vs zen_2

**Zen Technologies Limited** | Earlier: zen_1 | Later: zen_2

## Bottom Line

Between Q1 and Q2 FY26, earnings recovered sequentially (standalone revenue up about 12%, PAT up about 24%) with margins steady near 34%, but the order book weakened. It fell from about Rs.754 Cr to under Rs.675 Cr, with Rs.94 Cr of Q2 order wins coming mostly from subsidiaries. The Rs.650 Cr simulator order promised by September slipped to an uncommitted H2, ADS emergency orders are still at tender stage, and FY26 is now explicitly muted for the whole year. Management retains the Rs.6000 Cr FY26-28 target and rules out FY29 spill-over, but the Q1 numeric guidance on subsidiary revenue and capex was not repeated, and the drone ambition was narrowed to ADS with a stronger Indian-IP narrative. The thesis now rests on FY27-28 order conversion.
